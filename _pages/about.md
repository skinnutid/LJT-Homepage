## About Me
I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.hkust.edu.hk/), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by [Professor Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree.

My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning**, with specific interests in:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
